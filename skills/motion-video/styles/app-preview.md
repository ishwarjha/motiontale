# Style: app preview

*Also known as: app preview, app demo video, device mockup video.*

A mobile app shown on a device frame, portrait, built from real app screenshots: the store preview and the social cut in one.

- **Use for:** an App Store or Google Play preview, a mobile feature launch, a 9:16 social post.
- **Length:** 15-30 s (App Store previews: 15-30 s). **Voice:** none. **Formats:** 9:16 first (886x1920 for App Store, default 1080x1920), 16:9 with the phone beside the headline.
- **Scenes:** hook (the app's best screen, headline above) → demo (taps and swipes on real screens) → morph (one screen becomes the next through a shared element) → proof → end card with the store badge (only the store's official badge file, if the brief supplies it; otherwise the store's name as text).
- **Music:** 110-125 BPM (default: the kit track).

## Device
A plain device outline drawn as a rounded rectangle (default: radius 48 px and bezel 14 px on a 1080-wide frame, scaled with the device) around the real screenshot; no photo-real device, no reflections. The screen content is only captured app screens.

## Motion grammar
- Taps: a small ring pops at the touch point on the beat; the screen changes on the next half beat.
- Swipes: the screen content slides with a spring under a still device.
- Headlines above the device, one per screen, rising from the mask line.

## Camera
The device stays still; the headline and the screens move.

## Colour meaning
From the app's own palette; the accent marks the tap.

## Banned
3D device rotation, glossy reflections, screens not captured from the app, a hand or finger (it will be wrong), status bars with a real person's data.
