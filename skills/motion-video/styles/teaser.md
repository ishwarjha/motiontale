# Style: teaser

*Also known as: product teaser, launch teaser.*

Shortest cut. Opens on the finished result, one idea, out.

- **Use for:** a post that has to stop the scroll, a changelog headline, the first reply under a launch.
- **Length:** 8-20 s (default 15). **Voice:** none. **Formats:** 9:16 and 16:9 from one timeline.
- **Scenes:** hook (the payoff itself, fully on screen at frame one) → demo or morph (the one action that got there) → proof (the number, from facts.md) → end card.
- **Music:** 115-125 BPM (default: the kit track); the drop lands on the proof.

## Canvas, type, UI
From the brand. Default: the editorial card's canvas and type, headline 120 px (default) in 16:9, 96 px in 9:16.

## Motion grammar
- Frame one is the payoff: the result screen or the number, readable at 320 px wide.
- Then rewind the idea: how it got there, in one action.
- A change every beat; no hold over one beat.

## Camera
One push toward the action, one pull back for the proof.

## Transitions
Morphs only; there is no room for a second idea.

## Colour meaning
The brand accent colour means the result. Nothing else uses it: headline accent words keep the serif italic, in ink.

## Banned
Logo intro, a setup scene, more than one feature, text that needs a pause to read, anything from the editorial banned list.
