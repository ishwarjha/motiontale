# Style: dark keynote

*Also known as: product reveal, keynote-style reveal.*

A stage-style film: one statement at a time, slow and large. Contrast comes from flat values, never from light effects. Values marked (default) are starting points to edit.

- **Use for:** an event opener, a big release, or a pinned brand post.
- **Length:** 30-60 s (default). **Voice:** none, music only. **Formats:** 16:9, then 1:1.
- **Scenes:** hook (one statement per bar) → demo (one crop, no cursor chase) → morph → proof → end card.
- **Music:** the kit track. Half-time feel: one change per bar, not per beat. The proof lands on the drop.

## Canvas
Flat ink #15201B (default: the studio's ink colour, already used in the shadows). It's one solid colour, which H.264 encodes cleanly and doesn't band the way a gradient does.

## Type
Manrope 800 (default), centred, one statement per screen, with warm white #F7F8F6 text. Headlines are 2 lines or fewer, set larger than editorial. One accent word each, in Instrument Serif italic in #0B8F63. Check that the green reads on the ink, and lighten it if it doesn't.

## UI
Real crops on a flat card one step lighter than the canvas: radius 20 and a 1px border. There's no shadow, because a shadow on dark reads as a glow, and a glow is banned. The product screenshot is the brightest thing on screen, so let it be the light.

## Motion grammar
- Text rises out of the mask line one word per beat, but each statement takes a bar and then clears. The first statement is already risen at frame one: it's the thumbnail.
- Only one thing moves at a time.
- Holds of 1.5 beats at most still apply. The slow camera push counts as motion.

## Camera
One slow push-in per statement, zoom in log space. No follows and no whips.

## Transitions
Morphs. The accent word shrinks into a dot, and the dot grows into the next card.

## Colour meaning
Green means good news. #C24A1B is only for "late", and only if it reads on the ink. If it doesn't, drop red from this style.

## Banned
Everything on the editorial list. Also banned: vignettes, spotlights, light leaks, bloom, lens flares, gradient backgrounds, and any shadow or outer blur. Dark UI screenshots are banned unless the product really is dark.

## Dark scenes
The editorial card bans dark scenes for its own brand, not for every film. This card keeps every other rule. If a draft needs a glow to separate things, the layout is wrong: add space or scale instead.
