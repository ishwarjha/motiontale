# Style: sizzle reel

*Also known as: highlight reel, hype video, promo reel.*

A fast, high-energy cut of the best real moments: product highlights, real numbers, press and customer quotes, edited to feel like momentum building.

- **Use for:** a milestone or launch-week recap, an investor or partner pitch opener, an event opener, a "what we shipped this year" reel.
- **Length:** 45-90 s (default 60). **Voice:** none; quotes are on screen. **Formats:** 16:9 first, 9:16 cut.
- **Scenes:** hook (the strongest moment first: an image or a line, not the biggest number, which waits for the drop) → demo (3-6 real UI highlights, one per bar) → stat-card (each real number) → proof (the biggest number, on the drop) → endcard.
- **Music:** 120-130 BPM (default: the kit track), rising energy; the biggest number lands on the drop.

## Content rule
Every highlight is a real capture; every number and every quote has a `facts.md` row (`fact`: the quote's author and where it was published, the number's source and date). No invented press, logos or testimonials.

## Canvas, type
From the brand. Default: the editorial card's canvas and type. Quotes in the serif accent face, attributed on screen.

## Motion grammar
- One highlight per bar, cut on the downbeat through a shared shape (never a hard cut or fade).
- Acceleration: bars shorten toward the drop (4 beats, then 2, then 1), then one full-bar hold on the proof.
- Numbers count to their real value, whole values only.

## Camera
A push on every highlight, a still frame for every quote and number.

## Colour meaning
The accent marks the numbers and the proof; everything else is neutral.

## Banned
Invented or unattributed quotes, logos without permission, stock footage presented as the product, flashes, glows, whip-pans with blur, any hold over a beat and a half except the proof.
